Alienation Squared

On Double Expropriation and Dissociation as Structural Response in Late Capitalism

Piergiorgio Maruotti

Journal of Theoretical and Philosophical Psychology

Read manuscript (PDF) Official publication ↗

Overview

This article examines dissociation and depersonalization through the lens of contemporary alienation, proposing the concept of double expropriation to describe a condition in which individuals become estranged not only from their social and material existence, but also from their own subjective experience.

It introduces the notion of third-person subjectivity to explore the relationship between dissociative experience, identity, and the structural transformations of late capitalism.
