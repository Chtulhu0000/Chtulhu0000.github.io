Assessing Mental Health Therapeutic Capacity in AI Agents

Piergiorgio Maruotti et al.
Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (AIME 2026)
Springer · Proceedings, Part I · pp. 384–394

View publication ↗

Research overview

This paper investigates the capacity of artificial intelligence agents to perform tasks related to mental-health-oriented interactions. It examines how agent configurations and coordination strategies affect performance in psychological and conversational evaluation tasks.

The study contributes to research on large language models, multi-agent systems, and computational approaches to psychological assessment.
